## What we are asking you to do

Record three short spoken items (about 15 minutes total): one fixed sentence,
one short read passage, and one spontaneous answer of roughly 20 seconds. We
record each on a phone microphone and a laptop microphone, and one item over a
live phone call.

## What we will do with your voice

**We will make synthetic copies of your voice.** Your recordings are used as
reference audio for these voice-cloning tools:

- **XTTS-v2** (Coqui)
- **OpenVoice v2**
- **RVC** (Retrieval-based Voice Conversion)

Each produces audio that sounds like you saying the recorded sentences. We use
your real recordings and these synthetic copies together, as test data, to
measure whether our detector can tell them apart.

**We do not** make your voice say anything you did not record. The synthetic
clips reproduce the same sentences you spoke.

## Risks, stated plainly

A synthetic copy of your voice will exist. If it were misused it could be used
to impersonate you. We store recordings and clones on [[STORAGE — encrypted
drive / institutional account]], accessible to the named team only. We will not
place your clones on a public website, social media, or any voice-cloning
service's own servers beyond what generating the clip requires.

## Your rights

- Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any point without giving a reason.
- You may withdraw until **[[DATE — e.g. the corpus release date]]**. On

  versions but cannot recall copies already distributed.
- You may hear your clones before deciding on the release option above.
- You may ask what we hold about you and have errors corrected.
